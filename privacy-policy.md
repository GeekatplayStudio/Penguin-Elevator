# Privacy Policy — Penguin Elevator

Last updated: August 19, 2026

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal data.**

The App does not require account registration, login, or any personal information to play.

The App saves your game progress (high scores, level progress) locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to any server or third party.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 or any users.

## Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be reflected in updated versions of the App.
