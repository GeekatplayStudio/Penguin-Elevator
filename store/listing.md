## App name (limit 30)
Penguin Elevator

## Short description (limit 80)
A calm stealth puzzle. Drop the penguins only when nobody is looking.

## Full description (limit 4000)
The elevator is rising. Penguins keep getting on. Your job is to quietly get
rid of them - by opening a trapdoor under one, at the exact moment no other
penguin can see it happen.

Every penguin watches the floor in front of it: three tiles straight ahead,
two along each diagonal, and the single tile directly beside it. Behind them
is a blind spot. So is anywhere a fellow penguin is standing in the way. Read
the room, find the one passenger nobody is looking at, and tap.

Get it wrong and the witness jumps up with three red exclamation marks, and
the ride is over.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

- Every board is solvable. A solver checks the elevator after every boarding
  and every turn, and refuses any arrangement that could trap you. You never
  lose to an impossible deal - only to your own reading.
- A curve that respects you. The first twenty floors keep at least four safe
  drops available at all times, easing to three by floor fifty. It gets harder
  the way a good puzzle should: slowly, and never in a jump.
- No timer panic. Floors are long, rotations are rare and telegraphed, and
  rest floors give you room to breathe. This is a game to think in.
- Fish. Every ten floors you earn one. Drop it on an empty tile and every
  penguin turns to stare, leaving their backs wide open.
- Open ended. Climb as far as you can. Your best score and best floor are kept
  on your device.

PRIVACY, PLAINLY

No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts, no sign-up, no email. The game makes
no network requests at all - it does not even hold the internet permission, so
it cannot reach a server even in principle. Nothing about you leaves your
phone. Your high score is stored locally and disappears when you uninstall.
